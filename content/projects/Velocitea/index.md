## Overview
Everything moves and bounces in this Alien Bubbletea Cafe!
Juggle all the customers' orders by flinging your cups around the cafe to collect the right ingredients and deliver it to the correct alien!
Get the highest score you can in 3 minutes!

We were a big team of 8 people and I was **one of the 3 programmers** working on this **48h game jam project**.
