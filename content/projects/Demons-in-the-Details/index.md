## Overview

You play as an exorcist, who is undercover at a cult's ball and has to kill 3 demons with wine spiked with holy water. 
The problem? Everyone present is masked, and the significant features that should make up your targets are all mixed up due to a mistake in the information chain. 
The time to find the demons and exorcise them is short and your holy water is limited.


A 48h project for the Global Game Jam 2026.
Wanted to use it to try out Godot engine :)
